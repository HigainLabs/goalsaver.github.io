# Privacy Policy  
**Last Updated:** July 18, 2026  
**Developed by:** HiGain Labs Tech Pvt. Ltd.

Goal Saver Up (formerly GoalSaver, “the App”) is a goal-tracking application created by **HiGain Labs Tech Pvt. Ltd.** (“we”, “our”, “us”).  
This Privacy Policy explains how your data is handled when you use the App.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

**Goal Saver Up does *not* collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information.**

We do **not** collect:

- Names  
- Emails  
- Contacts  
- Financial data  
- Goals or entries  
- Device identifiers  
- Location information  

Any analytics collected by the Apple App Store (such as crash logs or usage data) is handled **solely by Apple**, not by us.

---

## 2. Use of Information

Because we do **not** collect any data:

- We do not track you  
- We do not share data  
- We do not use data for ads  
- We do not sell data  
- We cannot access any data you enter in the App  

All your entries (goals, income, expenses, currency preference, etc.) remain stored **only on your device**.

---

## 3. In-App Purchases and Subscriptions

The App offers optional paid features (“Goal Saver Up Pro”) as auto-renewable subscriptions and a one-time lifetime purchase.

- All payments are processed entirely by **Apple** through your Apple Account.  
- We never receive or store your payment details (such as card numbers or billing address).  
- Apple provides us only anonymized transaction records (for example, that a subscription was purchased or renewed) for accounting purposes. These records contain no personal or financial details about you.  
- Purchase entitlements are verified on your device using Apple’s StoreKit framework.

For how Apple handles your payment information, see Apple’s Privacy Policy.

---

## 4. Notifications

The App can send optional reminders about your goal deadlines.

- All notifications are **local**: they are created and scheduled entirely on your device.  
- No push-notification servers are involved, and no notification tokens or content ever leave your device.  
- Notifications are entirely optional and can be disabled at any time in the App’s settings or in iOS Settings.

---

## 5. Third-Party Services

The App uses **no third-party SDKs**, trackers, or external analytics tools.  
Only Apple’s built-in services (App Store, StoreKit for purchases, and local notifications) are used, under Apple’s policies.

---

## 6. Data Security

All data stays entirely **on your device**.  
We cannot view, retrieve, modify, or erase it.

If you delete the App, its data is removed with it. Standard Apple device backups (iCloud or computer backups) may include the App’s data under your own Apple Account, controlled entirely by you.

---

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated at any time, especially when new features or services are added.  
Any updates will be posted on this page.
